How to get your token

  1. Log in to your Injest web app
  2. Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12)
  3. Go to the Application/Storage tab
  4. Find "Local Storage" and select your app's domain
  5. Look for the "token" key and copy its value
  6. Paste it in the "Authentication Token" field above

Keyboard Shortcut

Press Cmd+V (Mac) or Ctrl+V (Windows/Linux) to capture clipboard content.

Note: The shortcut may need to be configured in Chrome's extension keyboard shortcuts settings.
